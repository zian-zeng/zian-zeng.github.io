EACL 2027

CallAdmit: Diagnosing and Efficiently Repairing Over-Abstention in Tool-Call Admission with Fixed-Proposal Certificates

Zian Zeng, Jordan Boyd-Graber

20th Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (EACL 2027). Reviewing through EACL 2027.

Action admission

Research focus

Analyzed 3,652 fixed proposed calls to distinguish admission checks’ activity from their contribution. Traceable normalization increased exploratory reduced-policy balanced accuracy from 78.9% to 88.4% on When2Call.

Download citation ↓
Budget reset
NAACL 2027

Restoration Is Not Neutrality: Auditing Visible Budget Controls in Agent Recovery

Zian Zeng, Furong Huang

2027 Annual Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (NAACL 2027). Reviewing through ACL Rolling Review.

Agent recovery

Research focus

Audited 60,720 recorded synthesis attempts and replayed 873 programs. A one-line ending cue restored Qwen performance across all 16 primary configurations while showing that visible-prefix controls are not equivalent to an invisible generation cap.

Download citation ↓
Goal feedback
COLING 2027

Correct Statuses, Wrong Targets: Truthful Goal Feedback Can Misdirect Stateful Repair Agents

Zian Zeng, Furong Huang

32nd International Conference on Computational Linguistics (COLING 2027). Reviewing through ACL Rolling Review.

Stateful repair

Research focus

Built paired repair experiments with 432 units per model and four prompt conditions. Traced misdirected edits to confusion between goal indices and record identities; object-keyed statuses removed index-named edits in pre-specified two-model probes. Reexecuted 183 programs from 72 trajectories.

Download citation ↓
Twin states
NAACL 2027

EffectTwin: Separating Recovery Limits from Output-Protocol Effects in Tool-Using Language Models

Zian Zeng, Ashok Agrawala

2027 Annual Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (NAACL 2027). Reviewing through ACL Rolling Review.

Recovery evaluation

Research focus

Built an exact belief-state solver and an independent certificate checker, establishing 199 recoverable and 100 unrecoverable cases in an authored finite model. Emitting actions before explanations improved next-action viability by 11.7–14.3 percentage points across two models.

Download citation ↓
State audit
NAACL 2027

The Agent or the World? Verifier Inflation in Persistent-World Agent Evaluation

Zian Zeng, Ashok Agrawala

2027 Annual Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (NAACL 2027). Reviewing through ACL Rolling Review.

Evaluation validity

Research focus

Across 318 runs and nine open-weight models, measured 6.9–19.6 percentage points of inflated success credit on exposed tasks and zero inflation in six structural-control cells. Separated agent work from useful state left by earlier tasks.
